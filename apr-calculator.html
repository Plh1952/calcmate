APR Calculator

See the true annual percentage rate (APR) of a loan once upfront fees are included. APR is higher than the nominal rate whenever a lender charges fees.

About the APR Calculator

APR is the rate that discounts the actual payment stream back to the money you effectively receive (loan minus fees). It makes loans with different fees comparable. A zero-fee loan has APR equal to its nominal rate.
