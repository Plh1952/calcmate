Auto Lease Calculator

Estimate the monthly payment on a car lease from the capitalized cost, the residual value, the money factor, and your down payment.

About the Auto Lease Calculator

A lease payment has two parts: depreciation (net cap cost minus residual, divided by the months) and a rent charge (money factor applied to the average of net cap cost and residual). Money factor × 2400 approximates the equivalent APR.
