Bond Calculator

Find a bond's yield to maturity from its price, coupon rate, face value, and remaining years.

About the Bond Calculator

YTM is the discount rate i solving Price = C×(1−(1+i)−n)/i + F/(1+i)n, with C the annual coupon and F the face value. Price below par means YTM above the coupon rate, and vice versa.
