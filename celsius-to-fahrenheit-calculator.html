Convert between Celsius and Fahrenheit temperatures in either direction.

About the Celsius to Fahrenheit Calculator

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Anchors: water freezes at 0°C / 32°F, body temperature 37°C / 98.6°F, boiling 100°C / 212°F.
