Concrete Calculator

Calculate how much concrete a slab needs: volume in cubic meters and yards, plus the number of bags.

About the Concrete Calculator

Volume = length × width × thickness. Bags: an 80 lb (36 kg) bag yields about 0.017 m³ (0.6 cu ft) of mixed concrete. Order or mix about 10% extra for spillage and uneven ground.
