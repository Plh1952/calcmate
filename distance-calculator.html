Distance Between Two Points Calculator

Find the distance between two points, in 2D or 3D, from their coordinates.

About the Distance Calculator

The distance formula comes from the Pythagorean theorem: d = √((x2−x1)² + (y2−y1)²), adding (z2−z1)² in 3D. Leave the z fields blank for 2D.
