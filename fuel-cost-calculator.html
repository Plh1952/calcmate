Fuel Cost Calculator

Estimate the fuel cost of a trip from the distance, your vehicle's consumption, and the fuel price.

About the Fuel Cost Calculator

Metric: fuel = distance × L/100km / 100. US: fuel = distance / MPG. Multiply by the price per liter or per gallon. Lower consumption saves twice: the same trip costs less and emits less.
