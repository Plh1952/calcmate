About the GPA Calculator

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits)/Σ(credits). Typical US scale: A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0. Courses with more credits move the average more.
