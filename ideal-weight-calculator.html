About the Ideal Weight Calculator

The Devine formula: men 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft; women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft. The BMI healthy range (18.5-24.9) is shown alongside because "ideal" formulas are population averages, not personal targets.

Informational only, not medical advice.
