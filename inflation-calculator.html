See how inflation raises the future price of goods and shrinks what your money will actually buy.

About the Inflation Calculator

Future cost = amount × (1+i)t; purchasing power = amount / (1+i)t. At 3% inflation, prices double roughly every 24 years, and idle cash loses half its real value over the same span.
