Simple Interest Calculator

Compute simple interest: interest earned on a principal at a fixed rate, with no compounding.

About the Simple Interest Calculator

Simple interest is I = P × r × t. Unlike compound interest, it never earns interest on interest, so it grows in a straight line. Many short-term instruments and installment plans use simple interest.
