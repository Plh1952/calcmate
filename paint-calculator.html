Paint Calculator

Estimate how much paint a room needs: walls and ceiling, minus doors and windows, times the coats.

About the Paint Calculator

Paintable area = 2 × (length + width) × height, plus the ceiling, minus about 2 m² per door and 1.5 m² per window. One liter covers roughly 10 m² per coat; porous or dark surfaces need more.
