About the Refinance Calculator

Refinancing trades your old rate and term for new ones. The break-even point is closing costs divided by monthly savings: if you will keep the loan past that many months, refinancing wins. Restarting the clock on a 30-year term can raise lifetime interest even when the monthly payment falls.
