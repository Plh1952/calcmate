About the Rent vs. Buy Calculator

Buying costs the mortgage payments but builds equity (home value minus remaining loan balance); renting costs the rent itself with no asset at the end. This simplified comparison ignores taxes, insurance, maintenance and transaction fees, which generally favor renting more than shown.
