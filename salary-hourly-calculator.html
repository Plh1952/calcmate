Salary to Hourly Calculator

Convert an annual salary to an hourly rate and back, using your weekly hours.

About the Salary to Hourly Calculator

Hourly = salary / (hours per week × weeks per year). The default 52 weeks assumes paid vacation; 50 weeks is closer to actual working time. A $52,000 salary at 40 hours per week is exactly $25 per hour.
