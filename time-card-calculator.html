Time Card Calculator

Calculate daily work hours from a start and end time, subtract the break, and optionally compute the pay.

About the Time Card Calculator

Hours are computed from the clock times; if the end time is earlier than the start, an overnight shift is assumed. The decimal hours are what payroll systems expect (7 hours 30 minutes = 7.5).
