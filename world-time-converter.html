About the World Time Converter

The Earth is divided into IANA time zones, each with its own offset and daylight saving rules. This converter applies the exact rules in force on the date you choose, so summer and winter are handled automatically. Fun fact: when it is 1 pm in Los Angeles, it is 10 pm in Paris the same day.
